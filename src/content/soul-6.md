The yarn now enters the weaver’s workshop.

The loom has been prepared.

The warp stretched.

The shuttle waits.

With every movement, warp and weft cross one another, gradually transforming thread into fabric.

The process is repetitive.

Yet no moment is mechanical.

Every movement demands attention.

By the end of many days—or sometimes weeks—the woven shawl finally emerges.

Inside modest workshops and family homes, artisans begin the painstaking task of transforming raw fleece into spinnable fibre.

Every coarse hair is removed.

Every impurity is carefully separated.

The cleaned fibre is combed repeatedly until it becomes soft, uniform, and ready for spinning.

This quiet work demands patience more than strength.

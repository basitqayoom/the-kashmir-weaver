This book is dedicated to the anonymous artisans of Kashmir—past and present—whose patience, creativity, and mastery have preserved one of the world’s greatest textile traditions.

It also acknowledges the contributions of historians, museum curators, conservators, researchers, shepherds, weavers, embroiderers, designers, and every individual who has helped protect this remarkable heritage.

May their work continue to inspire future generations.

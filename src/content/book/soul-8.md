The finished textile is inspected carefully.

Loose fibres are removed.

The surface is refined.

The fabric is washed and gently prepared for presentation.

Only after passing through these final stages is the shawl considered complete.

Its journey as a work of craftsmanship has ended.

Its journey through the world is about to begin.

As winter loosens its grip, the goats naturally begin to shed their undercoat.

Experienced herders carefully comb the loose fibre rather than simply removing it by force.

Each handful is precious.

The raw fleece contains both the fine underfibre and the coarser guard hairs.

Although it appears unremarkable at first glance, hidden within it is one of the world’s finest natural fibres.

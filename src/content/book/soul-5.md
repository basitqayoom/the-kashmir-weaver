The prepared fibre reaches the spinner.

Seated beside the traditional Yander, the spinner gently draws delicate fibres into an exceptionally fine yarn.

The wheel turns slowly.

The yarn grows almost invisibly.

Hours pass with remarkable concentration.

There are no shortcuts.

Every metre of yarn reflects the rhythm of experienced hands.

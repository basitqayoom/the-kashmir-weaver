From the High Himalayas to a Home Across the World

Every authentic Kashmiri pashmina begins its life long before it reaches a boutique, gallery, or collector.

Its story unfolds across mountains, rivers, workshops, and generations of skilled hands.

To understand the true value of a shawl, one must follow its entire journey.

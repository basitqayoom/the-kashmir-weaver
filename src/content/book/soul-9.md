Today, an authentic Kashmiri pashmina may travel thousands of kilometres.

It may arrive in London.

Paris.

New York.

Tokyo.

Dubai.

Sydney.

Or in the home of a collector who has searched patiently for authentic craftsmanship.

Its destination changes.

Its identity does not.
